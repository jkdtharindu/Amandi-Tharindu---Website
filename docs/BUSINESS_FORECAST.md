# Income Forecast, Market Reality and Growth Ideas

**Plain-language summary:** Your feeling is right. Most couples do not search for a wedding website, so this is a niche product. You will not make big money by waiting for demand. You can make a useful side income (and maybe more) by *creating* demand through photographers, planners and your own guests. The numbers below are **guesses built on stated assumptions**. They are meant to show you the shape of the business, not to promise income. Test the first 3 months, then replace the guesses with real numbers.

Forecast starts at the public launch (Mar 2027) in the plan in `BUSINESS_LAUNCH_PLAN.md`.

---

## 1. How big is the market?

- **Weddings:** Sri Lanka's marriage registry files show roughly 170,000 to 190,000 marriage records a year in the late 1990s and early 2000s ([Department of Census and Statistics catalogue](https://nada.statistics.gov.lk/index.php/catalog/383)). I found no recent official total, so I assume **about 130,000 to 160,000 weddings a year** today. Check this against the Registrar General's latest report.
- **Online reach:** about [13.9 million people used the internet at the end of 2025 (59.7%)](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-sri-lanka), and [Facebook and WhatsApp each have over 15.7 million users by one report](https://www.dailymirror.lk/business-news/Sri-Lankas-digital-engagement-hits-new-high-as-data-consumption-climbs-by-double-digits/273-329156). Your WhatsApp-based invitation idea fits how couples and guests already communicate.
- **What couples spend on:** I found no reliable price list for photography and decoration. Hall and catering costs are large (hotel packages around [LKR 6,500 to 10,000 per person](https://www.yamu.lk/yamu-guide/11-of-the-best-wedding-venues-in-sri-lanka/)). Against that, a LKR 20,000 to 35,000 website is a small extra, but it is not on most couples' list.
- **Competition:** few local, packaged services; mostly [freelancers on Fiverr at about $200 to $400](https://fiverr.com/dev_dileesha/do-wedding-websites-with-rsvp) and free global tools like Joy and Zola.

**What this means:** the market is big (over 100,000 weddings), but the share who want a wedding website today is small. Getting even 0.1% of weddings (about 150 couples a year) would be a good result.

## 4. Warning signs and checkpoints

Test early so you don't spend a year on a weak idea.

| Checkpoint | Good sign | Bad sign | What to do if bad |
|---|---|---|---|
| End of May 2027 (3 months) | 5 or more paying couples | 3 or fewer | Talk to 10 couples who said no. Ask why. Change the offer, price or channel |
| End of Aug 2027 (6 months) | 15 or more couples and 3 vendor partners | Under 8 couples | Stop paid ads. Focus on vendor partners only |
| End of Dec 2027 (9 months) | 35 or more couples and testimonials | Under 20 | Decide: keep as a small side business, or sell through partners only |

**Rule:** don't judge the product before 3 months of real selling. Don't keep spending on ads that don't bring orders.

## 5. Why couples may not buy (and what to do)

| Reason | What to do |
|---|---|
| They don't know the service exists | Show a live demo link, a short video and real guests' quotes |
| They spend on photos, hall and decoration first | Sell it as a small add-on that saves time and money (printing, postage, phone calls). Show the saving in rupees |
| They trust paper cards and phone calls | Offer a *paper plus digital* mix. Print a QR code on the paper card |
| Older relatives don't use smartphones | Keep a simple link plus WhatsApp. Offer a printed seating list for the day |
| It seems expensive | Start with a small Basic package and a first-customer discount |
| They don't want to learn a tool | Your Managed package: you set it up, they only handle guests and messages |

## 6. Growth factors (biggest first)

1. **Vendor partners** (photographers, planners, decorators, card printers). They already meet couples and have trust. A 10% referral fee is a cheap way to reach them. Aim for 3 active partners by Sep 2027.
2. **Your own wedding.** About how many guests attend? Each guest is a possible future customer. Add a small "Made by" line with your business link at the bottom of the site (with your guests' comfort in mind).
3. **Real testimonials and a live demo.** The strongest proof is a real couple's real result.
4. **Sinhala and Tamil.** A big part of the market may not choose an English-only site.
5. **Simple setup and reliable support.** Fast answers on WhatsApp keep couples happy and bring referrals.
6. **Seasonal timing.** Push your ads 3 to 6 months before busy wedding months.
7. **Social proof on Facebook and Instagram** with short videos.

## 7. Possible new products (later, once the first product sells)

Start from what couples already buy, and add on in small steps. Don't build these until the core product has paying customers.

| Idea | What it is | Why it could work | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Add-on packs** | QR code cards, printed seating charts, thank-you pages | Easy extra income from the same couple | Year 1 |
| **Save-the-date and engagement page** | A simple one-page site before the wedding | Earlier sale, lower price, more couples | Year 1 |
| **Photo and video sharing page** | Guests upload photos from the wedding; the couple gets an album | Popular with couples, keeps the site useful after the day | Year 1 |
| **Gift and wishes book** | Guests leave wishes; optional digital gift list | A good talking point; keep payments simple | Year 1 to 2 |
| **Vendor dashboard** | A page for photographers or planners to manage all their couples | Sells to businesses, not just couples; repeat income | Year 2 |
| **Other events** | Birthdays, engagements, baby showers, anniversaries, funerals' announcements, school events | Same product, a bigger market, events happen all year | Year 2 |
| **Wedding directory** | A list of local vendors with a paid listing | Income from vendors; needs lots of traffic first | Year 2 or later |
| **White-label for planners** | Planners sell the site under their own brand | Gets the sales done by someone else | Year 2 |
| **Overseas Sri Lankan couples** | Couples abroad planning weddings at home | Higher budgets, comfortable with online tools | Year 2 |

**Best next step after the core product:** the *photo sharing page* and *other events*. They need little new work and widen your market.
