# Who to Sell To, and Where to Find Them

**Plain-language summary:** Don't try to sell to every couple. Sell first to **young, online, city and town couples, engaged or about to be**, and reach them mostly **through the people they already trust**: photographers, planners, card printers and bridal shops. That is cheaper and closes faster than ads or marketplaces. Rural couples and older families can come later, through the same partners and with a simpler product.

(Everything here is a strategy guess to test in your first 3 months. See `BUSINESS_FORECAST.md` for the checkpoints.)

---

## 1. Your ideal first customer

| Trait | Best fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Age | 25 to 35, both partners | Comfortable with phones, apps and WhatsApp |
| Place | Colombo, Gampaha, Kandy, Galle, Kurunegala, Negombo, Jaffna and other larger towns | Better internet, more wedding spending, more vendors |
| Work | Office, banking, IT, teaching, health, business owners | Busy, want to save time |
| Relationship | Love marriage or couples who plan together | Both will use the app, as you said |
| Guests | 200 or more guests, or relatives living abroad | Tracking replies by phone is painful; guests abroad can't be reached by paper |
| Budget | Mid to upper wedding budget | A small add-on cost is easy to accept |

**Best single group: couples with many relatives abroad.** A website lets them see the details, RSVP and watch the day, so the value is easy to explain.

**Do not target yet:** rural areas, older couples and arranged marriages where parents run the invitations. They have less online habit and need hand-holding. Reach them later through a "family version" and a printed QR card.

---

## 2. Positioning: what to say

Don't sell "a website". Sell **the problem it solves**.

- **Main promise:** "Know exactly who is coming, without a single phone call."
- **Three supporting points:**
  1. Every guest gets a personal invite link and code.
  2. Bride's side and groom's side manage their own guests privately.
  3. WhatsApp reminders and a seating plan in one place.
- **Proof:** your own wedding, real guests' quotes, a live demo.
- **Avoid:** technical words. Say "invitation page" not "web app".

---

## 3. Who sells it best (ranked by efficiency)

| Rank | Channel | Why it works | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Wedding photographers and videographers** | They meet couples early, are trusted, and already deliver a "digital" product | 10% referral fee; give them a free demo site; offer the site as part of their package |
| 2 | **Wedding planners and coordinators** | They already manage guest lists and seating | White-label or referral deal; they use your Managed package for every client |
| 3 | **Invitation card printers** | The couple orders cards first; a QR code on the card links to the site | Offer a bundle: printed card with QR plus the website |
| 4 | **Bridal shops, dressmakers, salons and jewellers** | Couples visit them as soon as the date is fixed | A small counter card with a QR to your demo |
| 5 | **Hotel and hall coordinators** | They hand out vendor lists | Ask to be on the preferred-vendor list |
| 6 | **Your own guests and friends** | Warm trust, fastest sale | A short message after your wedding |
| 7 | **Facebook and Instagram ads** | Reaches many people, but cold and costs money | Use after partners are working, with a small budget |
| 8 | **Open marketplaces** (Fiverr, classifieds) | Price competition and low trust | Last. Only as a side channel |

**Why partners beat marketplaces:** a marketplace shows you next to cheaper sellers and free tools. A photographer's recommendation arrives with trust and no price comparison.

## 4. Facebook and Instagram boosting

**Check this first:** Meta's targeting options change often. One source reports that [Meta removed many interest options in January 2026](https://adlibrary.com/posts/meta-interest-targeting-removed). I could not confirm whether "recently engaged" or "newly engaged" life-event options are still available in Sri Lanka. **Open Ads Manager and look in the targeting menu before planning around them.**

**If "recently engaged" is available:**
- Location: your target cities
- Age: 24 to 35
- Life event: Recently engaged (and Newlywed, to catch couples still planning)

**If it is not available** (likely, plan for this):
1. **Location and age** (24 to 35, target cities).
2. **Interests** that remain, such as weddings, bridal fashion, wedding photography, bridal shows.
3. **Engage-and-retarget:** show a short video to a broad audience, then re-show your offer only to people who watched most of it.
4. **Lookalike audience** built from your paying couples (needs about 50 to 100 customers to work well).
5. **Click-to-WhatsApp ads:** the ad opens a chat with you. This fits Sri Lankan habits and gives you a real lead, not just a click.
6. **Boost posts on vendor pages:** ask a photographer to share your demo post to their page. Their followers are exactly engaged couples.
7. **Ask in the ad copy.** Use a clear hook such as "Getting married in 2027?" to filter the audience yourself.

**Budget advice:** start with a small test (for example LKR 1,500 to 3,000 a week), measure the cost of each WhatsApp enquiry, and only then increase it.

**Tagline ideas** (English; have a native speaker polish Sinhala and Tamil versions):
- "Getting married? Know who's coming before the big day."
- "One link. Every guest. No phone calls."
- "Your wedding invitation, on every guest's phone."
- "Bride's side and groom's side, managed separately."
- "Seat 300 guests in minutes, not weeks."

**Posting rules:** use a vertical video under 30 seconds; show a real phone screen; end with one clear step ("Message us on WhatsApp for the demo").

---

## 5. Reaching rural and older families (later)

Don't spend ads on them now. When ready:
- Offer a **printed QR card** and a **simple "one-page" site**.
- Let a **family member in town** (a son, daughter or cousin) set it up.
- Work through **local card printers and photographers** in those towns.
- Use **Sinhala and Tamil** wording and voice notes on WhatsApp.

---

## 6. First 90-day test

| Week | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 to 2 | Visit or message 10 photographers and planners with a demo link |
| 3 to 4 | Sign 3 referral partners. Offer the first 3 couples a discount for a testimonial |
| 5 to 8 | Post 2 videos a week. Run one small click-to-WhatsApp ad test |
| 9 to 12 | Count: enquiries, orders, cost per enquiry. Compare each channel |

**Decision:** put more effort into whichever channel gives the most paid couples per rupee and per hour. Drop the rest.
