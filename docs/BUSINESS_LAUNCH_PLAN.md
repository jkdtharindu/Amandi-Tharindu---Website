# Business Launch Plan: Selling the Wedding Invitation Website

**Plain-language summary:** First we launch your own wedding site (about 3 Nov). While the wedding happens, only small prep work runs. After the wedding we turn the site into a product other couples can buy, build the marketing, and start selling in January. Every task below has a deadline and a "done when" line, so you know when it is finished.

Dates assume today is **20 Oct 2026**, guest launch is about **3 Nov**, and the wedding is the **second week of December**. Change the dates if your plan moves.

How to use this file: change `[ ]` to `[x]` when a task is done. Do the tasks in order within each phase.

---

## Risks to watch

- **Setup time grows.** Each separate copy needs setup and care. If it takes longer than 2 hours, move to the "one system for many couples" model.
- **Guest data.** You will hold names and phone numbers. Delete it after each wedding.
- **Wedding month overload.** Do not start Phase 3 before the wedding is over.
- **Overpricing or underpricing.** Fix it after the first 3 customers, not before.
- **Copying.** Others can copy a site's look. Your edge is local fit, WhatsApp, local languages and personal service.
