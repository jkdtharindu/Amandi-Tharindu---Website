# Brand Profile: Business Name, Look and Campaign

**Decision:** the business name is **Aradhana** (Sinhala for "invitation"; say "ah-RAH-dha-na"). It was chosen on 20 Oct 2026. The name is **not yet checked** for legal or domain availability. Check the steps in section 2 before you print or pay for anything. Sections 1 and 1b below are the earlier name discussion, kept for reference.

**Known weak points of Aradhana, to manage:** it is also a common word and name in India (so Google results will be mixed, and trademark clashes are possible), and it is Sinhala, so Tamil-speaking customers may find it less natural. Always write "Aradhana Sri Lanka" or "Aradhana.lk" in posts, and consider a Tamil line (for example "Wedding invitations for families") on materials for Tamil audiences.

## 1. Name shortlist

| Rank | Name | Meaning | Good | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **lagna** | The auspicious wedding moment (Sinhala and Tamil) | Local, short, easy to say, fits both communities | Search results are mostly Indian (Marathi "Lagna Patrika") invitation templates, so it will be harder to rank on Google. Use "lagna.lk" and a Sri Lanka tag in all posts |
| 2 | **ekata** | "Together" (Sinhala) | Soft, modern, shows the "two sides, one wedding" idea | A US identity-verification company also uses the name Ekata (verify this yourself). Likely domain clash |
| 3 | **saha** | "and" (Sinhala) | Very short, fits "Amandi *and* Tharindu" | So short that it is hard to search and may clash with other uses |
| 4 | **vowly** | English, from "vows" | Fits Gen Z, global feel | Less local. Possible clashes with international brands |
| 5 | **kalyana** | Wedding / auspicious (Sinhala, Tamil) | Meaningful, well known | Long, formal, older feel |

**Searches that found no Sri Lankan brand using "Lagna", "Ekata" or "Saha" as a wedding invitation service** (these were quick web searches only, not a legal check). Don't treat that as clearance.

Why **lagna** wins: it is local and shared across both main communities, it is short for a Facebook handle and a QR code, and it suits a modern, simple design. If you think it sounds too traditional for Gen Z, the logo and tagline below make it look modern.

**Tagline options** (for Aradhana, "Wedding invitations for families" is also a strong fit):
- "Smart wedding invitations, made in Sri Lanka." (clear and direct, used in the logo)
- "Know who's coming."
- "One link. Every guest."

## 1b. Round 2: names with family and tradition (after your feedback)

You are right that **lagna** points to the horoscope and astrology side of a wedding. It misses the family, society and relationship feeling. These names aim at that feeling. Meanings are my understanding. **Have a native Sinhala and Tamil speaker confirm each one.**

| Rank | Name | Meaning | Feeling | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sambandha** | Relationship, connection, the tie between two families (the word is used for marriage alliances in Sinhala; Tamil has a close form, "sambandham") | Family, tradition, society | Longer, three syllables. Say it "sum-BUN-dha" |
| 2 | **Sangama** | Union, coming together (shared in Sinhala and Tamil) | Gathering of families, modern | Used as a word in other places and languages |
| 3 | **Pawula** | Family (Sinhala) | Warm, simple, very Sri Lankan | Sinhala only. A very common word, so the domain may be taken |
| 4 | **Aradhana** | Invitation (Sinhala) | Direct, matches your product | Also a common word in India; weak for Tamil speakers |
| 5 | **Sathkara** | Welcome, hospitality (Sinhala) | Honouring guests | Formal and long |

Quick searches found **no Sri Lankan wedding invitation brand using Sambandha, Sangama, Pawula or Aradhana**. This is not a legal check.

**Recommendation:** **Sambandha**, with the line "Wedding invitations for families." It carries the family and relationship meaning you asked for, and it suits both bride's side and groom's side.

**Keep "Lagna" for a future feature:** a page that shows the auspicious wedding time (nekath) for the ceremony. That fits the name's real meaning.

## 2. Before you commit to a name (do these in order)

1. Search the name on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Google, with "Sri Lanka" and "wedding".
2. Check domains: `aradhana.lk`, `aradhana.app`, `getaradhana.com`, `aradhanaweddings.lk`. A `.lk` domain is registered through LK Domain Registry resellers.
3. Check that the same handle is free on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.
4. Ask a native Sinhala and Tamil speaker how the name sounds and whether it has any unwanted meaning.
5. Check trademarks: search the National Intellectual Property Office of Sri Lanka (NIPO) records. Register the business name if the search is clear. Ask a lawyer before you pay for a trademark.
6. If any of these fail, move to the next name on the list.

## 3. Look and feel

**Mood:** warm, clean and modern. Calm, not flashy. It should feel like a premium app, not a cheap card template.

**Colours**

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Main dark | Ink | `#1F1B2E` |
| Background | Ivory | `#FBF7F2` |
| Accent | Gold | `#C8963E` |
| Soft accent | Blush | `#E8A9A0` |
| Support text | Warm grey | `#7C6F64` |

**Fonts:** a classic serif for the name and headlines (for example **Playfair Display**) and a clean sans-serif for body text (for example **Inter**). Both are free on Google Fonts.

**Logo concept:** two interlocking rings, one gold and one dark, with a small blush dot where they meet. The rings stand for the **bride's side and the groom's side joined**, which is also what your product does. The wordmark is lowercase "aradhana".

Files (open in a browser):
- `docs/branding/aradhana-logo.svg`: the full logo with tagline
- `docs/branding/aradhana-mark.svg`: the square icon, for profile pictures and the app icon

These are first-draft concepts made as simple vector files. Before you print, have a designer polish them (spacing, a custom font and a one-colour version).

## 4. Brand voice

- **Friendly and clear.** Short sentences. No jargon.
- **Confident, not pushy.** "Here is how it works" rather than "Buy now".
- **Warm and local.** Mention Sri Lanka, WhatsApp, families and weddings.
- **Say:** invitation, guest list, RSVP, seating plan, WhatsApp reminder.
- **Don't say:** web app, platform, SaaS, portal.

---

## 5. Campaign: "Know who's coming"

**Big idea:** the biggest wedding stress is not knowing who will turn up. We take that stress away.

**Launch sequence (2 weeks)**
1. **Teaser:** a close-up of a phone showing "You're invited". Caption: "Something new for Sri Lankan weddings. Coming soon."
2. **Problem post:** "300 guests. 300 phone calls. There is a better way."
3. **Demo video (20 to 30 seconds):** a guest opens their link, types their code, taps RSVP, and the couple sees the dashboard update.
4. **Proof post:** a real quote from a guest or couple (use your own wedding).
5. **Offer post:** "First 5 couples get launch pricing. Message us on WhatsApp."
6. **Partner post:** "Photographers and planners: earn 10% for every couple you refer."

**Content pillars (repeat every month):** how it works, real stories, wedding planning tips, behind the scenes, partner spotlights.

**Hashtags (test and adjust):** #aradhana #aradhanalk #srilankanwedding #weddingsrilanka #engagedsrilanka #weddingplanninglk
