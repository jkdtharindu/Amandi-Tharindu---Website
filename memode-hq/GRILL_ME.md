# Grill Me – Interview Routine

Triggers: "Grill me", "Interview me", "Challenge my thinking", "Let's define this properly", "Ask me everything necessary", "Let's finalise this decision".

## Rules
- 3–5 related questions per message. Most important first.
- Adapt to answers. Do not re-ask answered questions.
- Challenge weak reasoning, hidden assumptions, contradictions, unnecessary spend.
- Allow partial answers. Track unanswered ones and return to them.

## The 9 steps
1. Define the outcome (what decision or deliverable?)
2. List what is known and unknown
3. Ask prioritised questions
4. Adapt
5. Challenge assumptions
6. Summarise findings
7. Recommend the strongest option, with reasons
8. Ask for approval (HITL)
9. Update records: `DECISIONS.md`, `ASSUMPTIONS.md`, `TIMELINE.md`, `TASKS.md`

## Modes
Discovery · Grill · Strategy · Execution · Review · Decision. Say which mode you are in, and suggest a switch when it helps.

## Session 1 – Brand strategy (Phase 01)
Outcome: an approved brand foundation (purpose, customer, personality, "never be" list).

Round 1
1. What problem does MEMODE solve for a customer that existing brands in Sri Lanka do not?
2. Who is the first customer? (age, job, city, income, where they shop now)
3. Name three brands you admire. What exactly do you admire about each?
4. What should MEMODE never become?

Round 2
5. What should make MEMODE recognisable from across a room? (cut, colour, detail, fabric, fit)
6. "Accessible luxury" in LKR: what shirt price feels right, and what price feels wrong?
7. Do you still want three divisions at launch, or one range first? What is the reason?
8. How much of your own time can you give, and what do you do yourself vs. hire?

Round 3 (challenge)
9. What would make you stop MEMODE? What loss or result is the limit?
10. What proof of demand would you need before spending on stock?

## Session 2 – Product (Phase 05)
First garments? Fabrics? Target price range and quality bar? Fit needs for Sri Lankan sizing? Core replenishable items vs seasonal? How many styles and colours for the first test?

## Session 3 – Supply chain (Phase 06)
What do we outsource, and what do we control (sampling, QC, fit)? Capacity and MOQ needs? Backup suppliers? Which supplier risks worry you most?

## Session 4 – Finance (Phase 04)
Available capital? Acceptable loss? Monthly fixed costs? Minimum margin? Break-even target? Funding sources? Payback period expected?

## Session 5 – Technology (Phase 07)
Website must-haves? Payment methods? Inventory integration? What do you automate? What data stays under your control only?
