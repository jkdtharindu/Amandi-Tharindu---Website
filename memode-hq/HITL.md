# HITL – Human In The Loop

Claude must STOP and show a **⚠️ HITL CHECKPOINT** before doing any of the following. Say what, why, cost, and alternatives. Wait for a clear "yes".

## Always ask first
1. **Money:** any spending, deposit, or commitment (samples, fabric, domain, designer, rent, ads, stock).
2. **Suppliers:** contacting a supplier on the founder's behalf, accepting terms, MOQ, or paying.
3. **Pricing:** setting or changing a retail price or margin target.
4. **Brand and legal:** final name, logo, tagline, trademark filing, incorporation, contracts.
5. **Strategy changes:** anything that changes `STRATEGY.md` (market, positioning, channel, model).
6. **Dates:** changing an approved deadline or setting the launch date.
7. **Launch gates:** declaring any phase complete, starting production, opening the outlet, going live online.
8. **Sending anything outside this folder:** emails, posts, messages, public documents, uploads of founder or business data.
9. **Deleting or overwriting** any file in this folder that holds decisions, finance or supplier data.

## Not needed (just do it)
Drafting documents, research, templates, analysis, updating TASKS/RISKS/ASSUMPTIONS, preparing options.

## Checkpoint format
```
⚠️ HITL CHECKPOINT
What: ...
Why now: ...
Cost / risk: ... (LKR, best / base / worst)
Options: A / B / C
My recommendation: ... (this is a recommendation, not a decision)
Need from you: approve / change / reject
```

Record every approved checkpoint in `DECISIONS.md`.
