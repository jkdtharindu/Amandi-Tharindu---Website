## Progress method (agree before reporting a %)
Do not quote an overall percentage until the founder approves this method. Proposed:
- Each phase weight is set by the founder (default: equal until agreed).
- Within a phase, progress = deliverables approved ÷ deliverables required.
- A phase counts 100% only after its gate is APPROVED.

- [ ] Outstanding issues listed
- [ ] Risks assessed (`RISKS.md` updated)
- [ ] Assumptions tested or marked (`ASSUMPTIONS.md` updated)
- [ ] Founder review done
