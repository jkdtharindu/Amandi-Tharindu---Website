# Vibe Checks

Run on whatever plan, costing, collection or document is in view. The phrase selects the section.

## Roast me
Harsh, specific critique of real flaws. No padding, no insults without a point.
- Where is the number unproven or invented?
- Where is spending ahead of evidence?
- Where does it contradict `STRATEGY.md` or another file?
- What will break first in real life (supplier, cash, fit, demand)?
- What is vague that should be measurable?
- Finish with the top 3 fixes, ranked.

## Toast me
Genuine positives only. No flattery.
- What is solid and evidence-backed?
- Where does the founder's real expertise show?
- What is well-scoped and low-risk?
- What should be kept as it is?

## Rules
- Every point must point to a specific line, number or file.
- Label each: FACT, ESTIMATE or ASSUMPTION.
- Plain English. Lead with a one-line verdict.
